1. What problem does the app solve?
    Netflix is expensive, users need an affordable alternative
2. Who are the users?
    Busy people trying to relax at the end of the day.
3. What are the primary goals?
    Provide an affordable easy to use streaming service so users can enjoy their favorite tv shows and movies.
4. What information do they need?
    A selection of viewing options
5. What actions can they perform?
    Select something to watch
    View their watch history
    Favorite an option to have it display in a ‘Favorites’ area
6. What pages are required?
    A Sign-in/Sign-up page
    A selection page
    A details page to read about an item they are interested in watching
    A cinema page where the video plays
    A profile page where they can manage profile details/settings
7. What data must be collected?
    Email and Password at sign-up
    Authentication of Email and Password at sign-in
    Payment information if they are to be charged
8. What permissions exist?
    Users only see their own information
