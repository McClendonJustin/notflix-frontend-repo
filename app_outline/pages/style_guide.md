Style Guide - Notflix
---
Vibe: Form meets function, users are here to make a selection on something to watch, we want to present them with a large selection cleanly so they do not have to dig for something that peaks their interest.
Mobile-First: Design for a 390px phone when signing-in or making a selection, that will flip to 844px in landscape when watching.
Colors: Dark theme. Near-black background (#0b0c0d), off-white text (#f2f4f0), light-green accent (#b7e8bf). (Changed from the original chalk-white/iron scheme after Phase 1 review.)
Type: A highly readable sans font for body
Touch Targets: Poster cards are 7:10 (about 168 x 239px on desktop, 110px wide or more on a 390px phone); buttons and icon buttons are at least 44-48px. (Cards were shortened from the original 85 x 150px minimum after Phase 2 review.)
Motion: We should minimize unnecessary motion, but a smooth scrolling experience is imperative, when opening modals for details or profile settings, motion should be subtle and swift
Navigation: A navigation bar on the left hand side should be able to be suppressed/shown using a hamburger stack icon, users can view genre options here as well as a quick-link to favorites or history.
Accessibility: Visible keyboard focus when searching, Form fields use placeholders and aria-labels instead of visible labels, and each poster carries its own title as its label.
